Priority Actions

  1. Limit phone-number visibility to 'Only me' where possible.
  2. Avoid publicly broadcasting real-time location unless intentionally needed; share live location only with specific trusted people.
  3. Enable multi-factor authentication using the strongest supported method (security key or authenticator app over SMS).
  4. Share travel photos after you return, or limit travel posts to close friends.
  5. Post check-ins and photos after you have left the location ('late posting').
  6. Verify unfamiliar profiles before accepting connections; decline when unsure.
  7. Use a unique password for this account; change it now if it is reused elsewhere.
