Privacy ≠ security

A strong password doesn't hide your birthday

These are related but separate problems, so the framework scores both.

Privacy

Controls how personal information is collected, shared, exposed and used.

  • Who can see your phone number, birthday or city
  • Whether posts reveal where you are right now
  • What old posts and tags say about you

Security

Protects systems, accounts and information from unauthorised access or misuse.

  • Multi-factor authentication and unique passwords
  • Login alerts and reviewing active sessions
  • Refusing to share verification codes

An account can have MFA and a unique password and still publish a full birth date, a workplace and live location to everyone. Strong account security is not the same as strong privacy.

What the score is made of

Each category gets its own 0–100 score. Your overall score is the weighted average of the ten. The weights are teaching assumptions, not a validated model.

How an assessment runs

From answers to a report

  1. Questionnaire53 questions, answered with fixed choices
  2. ValidationOnly allowed answer codes are accepted
  3. Feature extractionEach answer becomes a 0–1 risk value
  4. Category scoringA weighted score for each of the ten categories
  5. Overall scoreA weighted average, then Low / Moderate / High / Critical
  6. FindingsRisky answers ranked by severity
  7. RecommendationsImmediate, important and good-practice fixes
  8. ReportPrintable HTML or PDF with no personal data

Privacy by design

What this app never collects

  • Phone numbers or email addresses
  • Home addresses or exact locations
  • Birth dates
  • Passwords or verification codes
  • Names, usernames or profile links
  • Your questionnaire answers (they are scored and discarded)

Results are saved only if you tick "Save my anonymous result". Even then, only scores and finding types are stored, and they are deleted after 30 days or whenever you delete them.

This project is for defensive cybersecurity and privacy education. It uses synthetic or voluntarily provided assessment responses and does not scrape, track or profile real social-media users.

The score is an educational estimate from self-reported answers. It does not guarantee that an account will or will not be compromised.
